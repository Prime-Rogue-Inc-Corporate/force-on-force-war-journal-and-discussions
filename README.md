# Force on Force: war journal and discussions

The war journal of [Force on Force](https://forceonforce.si), a simulator of a war between Canada and the
United States, by [Kevin Duska](https://kevinduska.com) of [Prime Rogue Inc.](https://primerogueinc.com)

The journal records cases where play or the model throws up something the rules do not cover, and the ruling
reached. Each case says what happened, what the question is, the argument, and the rule that follows.

## Taking part

Open cases are argued on the journal's [discussion board](https://github.com/Prime-Rogue-Inc-Corporate/force-on-force-war-journal-and-discussions/discussions). Each open case has a poll and a thread.
Vote, and say why: the reasons matter more than the count. Kevin decides, and the ruling records what the
discussion said and where it changed the outcome.

## Who writes this

Kevin Duska sets the direction and decides each case. The cases are written up with Claude, an AI system made
by Anthropic, which acts as the model judge: it proposes a ruling and argues for it. Where AI agents were used
to simulate press coverage or memes, the case says so, says how many, and says what they were told.

## What this is not

It is a simulation and a game. Nothing here is a prediction, a plan or advice. The press coverage and memes
are fiction, name no real person or outlet, and are marked as fiction.
