# Case 1: the poll

Two questions, each posted as a poll in this repository's Discussions tab. Vote, then say why in the thread.
The full case is in [`README.md`](README.md).

## Poll 1: who won?

**Title:** Case 1: Canada holds Sweet Grass, the US holds Lethbridge. Who won?

At the end of day 24 the US holds Lethbridge with 15 strength. Canada has abandoned the city, and its last
four counters sit in Sweet Grass, Montana, on Interstate 15, behind the US army. The rules as written say the
US wins and take no notice of Montana.

- A US victory, plain. Lethbridge is what counts.
- A marginal US victory. Lethbridge decides it, but a lost base and a cut supply road take the shine off. (The proposed ruling.)
- A draw. Each side holds something of the other's.
- A marginal Canadian victory. The US ran out of clock with a foreign flag in Montana.

## Poll 2: what does it do to American support for the war?

**Title:** Case 1: Canadian troops hold a US border post. What happens to US support for the war?

Assume nobody in Sweet Grass is hurt.

- It rises and stays up. An attack on American soil turns a war of choice into a war of defence.
- It rises for a few weeks and fades. (What seven AI agents writing as the press all said.)
- It falls. A president who cannot hold Montana looks weak.
- It barely moves. It is a truck stop.
